How we choose partners

Selected,
not sold.

I take a small number of partners, and I choose them against six things.

  • A credible business that can be checked
  • A market you understand, and why customers buy from you
  • An offer worth building on
  • Customers worth winning, where one new customer is worth a great deal
  • A team that responds fast and closes
  • Commitment to a long-term relationship, goals and honest review
  1. 01Apply. A short form, read by me personally.
  2. 02Review. I check the business and reply within 3 business days, either way.
  3. 03Conversation. If it looks right, I send you a booking link. Thirty minutes by video, and I ask more than you do.
  4. 04Fit decision. Is your territory open? I say yes, not yet or no, and why.
  5. 05Partner brief. A short written brief on your market, ideal customer and deal value becomes the targeting and qualification criteria.
  6. 06Ten-week blocks. SMART goals set up front. Every 10 weeks we review the metrics with me, and the results decide whether we scale up, change course or scale down.

How we measure

Three gates.
Held as targets.

These are our targets. They are not guarantees and they are not results we have achieved. We do not publish client results on this website.

Performance targets and how each is measured
GateTargetHow it is measured
Acceptance80% or moreOf the leads handed over, the share you accept as meeting the criteria we agreed. Each lead is recorded as accepted or rejected, and every rejection carries a reason.
First-response SLA90% or moreOf the leads handed over, the share where first contact is made within the response time we agree with you. Handover and first-contact times are timestamped.
ReplacementUnder 10%Of the leads handed over, the share that has to be replaced because it did not meet the agreed criteria.

Response speed is a shared gate: the first-response target depends on your team contacting the lead promptly. I review these numbers with you every 10 weeks.
