Kitchens, bathrooms and renovations

How it works for you.

For a renovation business, one good job can change the month. LeadPipes is a business-to-business service: you are our client, and we help you reach the right commercial and trade buyers, or enquiries that have asked to hear from businesses like yours.

Where a prospect is a business, such as a builder, developer, strata or property manager, we use publicly published business contact details and contact them in their business capacity. We do not buy homeowner lists or send unsolicited messages to consumers.

How opportunities are sourced

  • Inbound or opt-in enquiries where the person has asked to hear from businesses like yours
  • Publicly published business contact details of trade and commercial buyers, contacted in their business capacity
  • No cold contact of homeowners
  • Criteria that you and we agree in writing before we start

I work with a handful of serious operators: businesses where one good sale matters, with a team that responds fast and can close. We do not guarantee sales. We commit to researched, qualified, exclusive opportunities.
