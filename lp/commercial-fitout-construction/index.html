Commercial fit-out and construction

How it works for you.

Fit-out and construction work is won early, usually before a formal tender exists. The businesses planning a move, an expansion or a refurbishment are talking to someone. The question is whether it is you.

LeadPipes researches businesses showing those triggers, opens a business-to-business conversation with the person who decides, and qualifies the reply. Your team then takes the scoping, the quote and the project.

What we look for

  • Commercial triggers such as a move, expansion, new premises or refurbishment
  • Directors, property managers and operations leads who influence the project
  • A scope or timing clue we can show you as evidence
  • Criteria that you and we agree in writing before we start

I work with a handful of serious operators: businesses where one good sale matters, with a team that responds fast and can close. We do not guarantee sales. We commit to researched, qualified, exclusive opportunities.
