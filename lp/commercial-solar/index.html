Commercial solar and energy

How it works for you.

Commercial solar is a considered purchase. The person who signs off is rarely the person who answers the general enquiries line, and a quote only moves forward when the timing, the site and the budget line up.

LeadPipes does the front end of that work for you: researching which businesses are worth approaching, opening a business-to-business conversation with the right person, and qualifying the reply before you see it. Your team then owns the site assessment, the proposal, the close and the delivery.

What we look for

  • Commercial and industrial businesses that fit the sites and project sizes you want to quote
  • The person who decides or influences the spend: owner, director, facilities or operations lead
  • A reason to talk now, with evidence we can show you
  • Criteria that you and we agree in writing before we start

Disclosure: Darren Harry also operates SolarPipe, a separate commercial solar brokerage. Exclusivity under a LeadPipes agreement applies to the opportunities LeadPipes generates for you in your agreed category and state. We will explain on the discovery call how the two businesses relate, before you decide anything.

We want serious operators: businesses where one good sale matters, with a team that responds fast and can close. We do not guarantee sales. We commit to researched, qualified, exclusive opportunities.
