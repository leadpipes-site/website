B2B and professional services

How it works for you.

Professional services are bought on trust and timing. A cold list does not give you either. A researched account with a named decision-maker and a reason to talk does.

LeadPipes researches target accounts, contacts the relevant person in their business capacity with a message that is specific to their role, and qualifies the reply. You receive the context, the conversation so far and a recommended next move.

What we look for

  • Accounts that fit the size, sector and location you serve best
  • The person who owns the problem you solve, not a generic inbox
  • A reason your offer is relevant to them now
  • Criteria that you and we agree in writing before we start

I work with a handful of serious operators: businesses where one good sale matters, with a team that responds fast and can close. We do not guarantee sales. We commit to researched, qualified, exclusive opportunities.
