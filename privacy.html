Privacy policy

Last updated 16/10/2026.

Who we are

LeadPipes (leadpipes.com.au) is an Australian lead generation business. It is operated by [registered business name to be supplied], ABN [ABN to be supplied]. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “LeadPipes” mean that business.

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or to be removed from our contact lists, email enquiries@leadpipes.com.au.

Who this policy covers

  • People who visit this website or submit an enquiry or application form.
  • People who apply to work with us and book a conversation.
  • People at businesses whose publicly published business contact details we collect so that we can contact them business-to-business on behalf of our clients, and people who reply.

What we collect

When you submit a form on this website

Your name, business name, website, email, state, business category and what you want from us. Optionally: your phone number and, if you open “Tell us more”, your role, team size, years trading, marketing spend, average sale value, how many opportunities you want each month, where your leads come from today and your preferred call time. We also record the time of the enquiry.

When you book a conversation with us

Bookings are made through Calendly, a third-party service. Calendly collects the details you enter, such as your name, email address, chosen time and any answers you give. We receive those details. Calendly handles them under its own privacy policy as well.

Cookies and tracking on this website

This website does not set cookies and does not load third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. To record how you arrived, a small piece of text is saved in your browser’s storage: the campaign tags in the link you clicked (such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term), advertising click ids (gclid, fbclid, msclkid), the page you landed on, the website that referred you, and the time of your first visit. It stays on your device until you clear your browser data. It contains no name, email or other personal details, and is sent to us only if you submit a form, where it is stored with your enquiry. Forms also record how long the form was open, to help us block automated spam. Our website host may also process technical data, such as your IP address, when it delivers the site.

Business contact details we collect for outreach

To find businesses that may want our clients’ services, we collect publicly published business contact details: for example a person’s name, job role, business name, business website, work email address and business phone number, from the business’s own website or other public business sources. We record where and when we found each detail. We aim to contact people only in their business capacity and only where our message is relevant to their role. If you want to know where we found your details, ask us.

If you reply to us

We keep your replies and the conversation, and notes about how well the opportunity matches what our client is looking for.

Why we collect and use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, assess whether we can work together, and arrange a conversation.
  • To contact businesses by email in a business-to-business context, in line with the Spam Act 2003: our messages identify LeadPipes and include a working unsubscribe.
  • To qualify replies, and to deliver qualified leads to the client who has engaged us for that category and state (see the next section).
  • To see which marketing brings enquiries, to keep the website secure and to block spam.
  • To meet legal obligations and to handle complaints or disputes.

Who we share it with

Our clients, when we deliver a lead

We do share lead and contact details with the client who engages us. When a person at a business we have contacted replies and we qualify them as a potential customer for a client, we give that client the lead: the person’s name, role, business and business contact details, why the business appears to fit, and our notes and the conversation so far. We are paid by our clients for delivering qualified leads. The client we share with is the one we serve in that business’s category and state. The client will then contact the person about their products or services using its own processes. If you ask us not to be contacted, we record that and tell the client.

If you are applying to become a client

We do not sell the details you give us in an enquiry or application, and we do not pass them to our other clients.

Service providers

We use service providers to run the business. Today these include Netlify (website hosting and form handling), Google Workspace (email and documents), Calendly (bookings) and Railway (hosting for our customer relationship system, LeadDrive, where leads, contacts and conversations are stored). We may also use similar providers to host, store or send email on our behalf. They may only use personal information to provide their services to us.

Others

We may disclose information where the law requires or permits it, for example to a regulator or court, or to our professional advisers.

Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries. We do not control exactly where each provider processes data, and we take reasonable steps to choose providers that protect personal information.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including access controls and the security features of the services we use. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for [retention period to be confirmed]. If you unsubscribe or ask us not to contact you, we keep a minimal record of your email address and request so that we do not contact you again.

Access, correction, removal and unsubscribing

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing enquiries@leadpipes.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Every outbound email we send includes a working unsubscribe link. You can also reply “unsubscribe” or “remove me” to any message. We honour unsubscribe requests promptly, and no later than the law allows.

Complaints

Tell us first at enquiries@leadpipes.com.au and we will try to fix it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). For unwanted commercial messages you can also contact the Australian Communications and Media Authority (acma.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
