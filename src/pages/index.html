The LeadPipes model

From cold market
to warm handover.

A disciplined four-stage system, with human judgment protecting the quality at the point that matters.

01

Build the target

We define the territory, buyer, commercial triggers and exclusions before a single message leaves the system.

02

Research the account

We identify the right businesses and people, then add the context your sales team needs to sound informed, not automated.
