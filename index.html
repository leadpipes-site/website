The 10-week review

Every 10 weeks, the numbers decide.

We set SMART goals together before anything starts: specific, measurable, agreed in writing, with a date. Then every 10 weeks I sit down with you and we review the results against them. What the metrics show decides what happens next. Nothing runs on autopilot.

Measured
  • Opportunities delivered and accepted
  • First-response speed
  • Conversations, meetings and proposals
  • Closed customers and their value
  • Cost against return
Reviewed
  • What is working and what is not
  • Lead quality and messaging
  • Market and territory direction
  • Your team's response and conversion
  • Progress against each agreed goal
Decided
  • Scale up where the numbers earn it
  • Change what is underperforming
  • Scale down or stop where it is not working
  • New goals set for the next 10 weeks

Scaling up or down is a decision we make from the evidence, together, every cycle. If it is not working, I will say so.
